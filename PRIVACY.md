# Privacy policy

IG Plus is a browser extension for Instagram. It has no server, and its developer collects no data
about you or how you use it.

## What IG Plus handles

- **Settings.** "Hide home feed posts" and "Hide Reels" are saved in your browser's extension storage,
  which your browser may sync to your other devices.
- **Photos, videos and text for stories.** Webcam photos, files you choose, Google Photos items you
  pick, and the text and drawings you add stay in the page while you edit. They are sent only to
  Instagram, when you post the story, the same way Instagram's own site sends them.
- **Posts you share to a story.** IG Plus reads the post's image, video and author from Instagram to
  draw it into your story.
- **Google sign-in.** If you use Google Photos, Google signs you in and gives the extension a token
  that can only read the items you pick in Google's photo picker. The token stays in your browser and
  is sent only to Google.
- **Requests to Instagram.** In the IG Plus phone window, IG Plus changes the browser identity
  Instagram sees, so it offers story posting. When you share to close friends, skip Facebook, or link
  a shared post, it adds those choices to Instagram's request.

## What IG Plus does not do

- It does not send data to its developer or to anyone other than Instagram and Google, as above.
- It does not sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.
- It does not read your messages or other Instagram data beyond what is listed above.
